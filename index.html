Mexico City Trip

Mexico City Itinerary (Nov 1–9, 2026)

Base: Coyoacán Loft • Prepared for Family & Friends

Day 1

Sunday, Nov 1 • Arrival

Arrival Night
19:40

Land at MEX Airport. Uber or official airport taxi straight to the loft in Coyoacán.

Evening

Check-in & Late Comfort Bite: Settle in, then take a quiet walk to Plaza Hidalgo / Jardín Centenario for fresh tacos or churros.

Day 2

Monday, Nov 2 • Día de Muertos in Coyoacán

Holiday Altars
Morning

Neighborhood Coffee Walk: Grab coffee and pan dulce at Café El Jarocho. Stroll the tree-shaded paths of Viveros de Coyoacán and tranquil Plaza de la Conchita (no ticket queues or museum rush).

Afternoon

Mercado de Coyoacán: Taste tostadas and seasonal treats while admiring vibrant marigold ofrendas (altars).

Evening

✨ Open for Family & Friends: Stroll the illuminated plazas together to see the peak holiday atmosphere and public ofrendas.

Day 3

Tuesday, Nov 3 • Dress Shopping Day

Shopping Focus
Morning

Designer Boutiques: Head to Carla Fernández (Juárez) for handcrafted modern textiles, then Yakampot (San Miguel Chapultepec) for elegant, breathable occasion dresses.

Afternoon

Department Store Browse: El Palacio de Hierro Polanco (broadest variety under one roof for eveningwear, cocktail styles, and accessories).

Dinner

Antolina (Condesa): Casual, elevated dinner featuring chinampa produce and regional mezcal — or open for dinner with friends.

Day 4

Wednesday, Nov 4 • Centro Histórico

History & Courtyards
Morning

Historic Core: Zócalo, Metropolitan Cathedral, Templo Mayor ruins, and the golden interior of Palacio Postal.

Lunch

🍽️ Azul Histórico: Unwind in the stunning 17th-century open courtyard under the trees. Celebrated regional Mexican cuisine without tasting-menu pricing.

Evening

✨ Open evening for relaxed dinner with family or friends.

Day 5

Thursday, Nov 5 • Chapultepec & Condesa

Park & Museum
Morning

Anthropology Museum (MNA): One of the world's greatest museums, set inside Bosque de Chapultepec (2–3 hours at an easy pace).

Afternoon

Condesa Wander: Walk the lush perimeter of Parque México and quiet side streets for coffee or ice cream.

Dinner

Fonda Fina (Roma Norte): Cozy neighborhood fonda serving refined homestyle cooking and rich moles.

Day 6

Friday, Nov 6 • Excursion Day

Flexible Choice
Morning

Option A: Early trip to Teotihuacán Pyramids (leave by 07:30 to beat crowds).
Option B: Early visit to Central de Abasto market for an immersive food experience.

Afternoon

Return to Coyoacán base for rest, a shower, and zero hurry.

Evening

✨ Open evening with family & friends.

Day 7

Saturday, Nov 7 • San Ángel Arts

Boutique & Art Market
Morning

Bazar El Sábado: Celebrated open-air and indoor art market in San Ángel with silver jewelry, ceramics, and textiles (very close to Coyoacán).

Lunch

Courtyard dining around Plaza San Jacinto.

Evening

✨ Open evening for a family gathering in southern CDMX.

Day 8

Sunday, Nov 8 • Roma Stroll & Farewells

Relaxed Pace
Morning

Sunday Boulevard Walk: Stroll the shaded oval of Avenida Ámsterdam, visit bookstores, and enjoy relaxed sidewalk brunch.

Afternoon

Downtime at the loft, packing, and souvenir picking.

Evening

Farewell Dinner: Cozy group dinner and toast to a wonderful week.

Day 9

Monday, Nov 9 • Departure

Check-out
Morning

Final coffee in Coyoacán, pack bags, check out of loft, and transfer to MEX Airport.

Curated Dress Shopping Day (Tuesday, Nov 3)

A focused route balancing artisanal Mexican craftsmanship with comprehensive department store selection.

Carla Fernández

Calle Marsella 72, Colonia Juárez

Artisan Couture

Celebrated Mexican fashion house collaborating with indigenous weavers and master artisans. Perfect for modern, structured dresses, unique textiles, and statement silhouettes.

Area: Juárez / Roma border Open Map →

Yakampot

Salvatierra 45, San Miguel Chapultepec

Boutique

Understated, elegant, and fluid occasion dresses crafted in natural fibers. Celebrates Mexican textile heritage with clean contemporary tailoring.

Area: Near Condesa & Chapultepec Open Map →

El Palacio de Hierro Polanco

Av. Moliere 222, Polanco

Flagship Department Store

Known as "El Palacio de los Palacios". The best one-stop location if you need to try a high volume of formal, cocktail, and wedding-guest dresses across diverse brands and sizes.

Area: Polanco Open Map →

Approachable, Exceptional Dining

High-quality regional Mexican food and great atmospheres without tasting-menu pricing or rigid bookings.

Azul Histórico

Isabel La Católica 30, Centro Histórico

Mid-Range • Scenic

Located in the open-air courtyard of the Palacio de los Condes de Miravalle. Known for traditional Oaxacan moles, duck buñuelos, and tortilla soup under lighted trees.

Best for: Wed, Nov 4 lunch Open Map →

Antolina

Calle Vicente Suárez 116, Condesa

Mid-Range • Farm-to-Table

A warm neighborhood bistro sourcing ingredients directly from Xochimilco chinampas. Excellent plantain molotes, slow-cooked meats, and small-batch mezcals.

Best for: Tue, Nov 3 dinner Open Map →

Fonda Fina

Medellín 79, Roma Norte

Mid-Range • Classic Comfort

Reimagines Mexican fonda classics. Choose your protein and pair it with house moles, pipianes, and warm heirloom corn tortillas in an inviting dining room.

Best for: Thu, Nov 5 dinner Open Map →
