Nov 1 – 9, 2026 • 8 Nights

Mexico City Trip

Base: Pino 82, Coyoacán • Flexible Evenings

Day 1

Sunday, Nov 1 • Arrival

Arrival Night
19:40

Land at MEX Airport. Direct transfer to loft in Coyoacán (Pino 82).

Evening

Check-in & Late Comfort Bite: Settle in, then take a quiet evening walk to Plaza Hidalgo or Jardín Centenario for late casual street tacos and churros.

Day 2

Monday, Nov 2 • Día de Muertos in Coyoacán

Holiday Altars
Morning

Morning Coffee Walk: Grab coffee and warm pan dulce at Café El Jarocho. Stroll tree-shaded paths through Viveros de Coyoacán and tranquil Plaza de la Conchita (no ticket queues or museum rush).

Afternoon

Mercado de Coyoacán: Taste tostadas and seasonal snacks while admiring vibrant marigold ofrendas (altars).

Evening

✨ Open for Family & Friends: Stroll the illuminated plazas together to see peak holiday atmosphere and public ofrendas in Coyoacán or Centro.

Day 3

Tuesday, Nov 3 • Dress Shopping Day

Shopping Focus
Morning

Designer Boutiques: Head to Carla Fernández (Juárez) for handcrafted modern textiles, then Yakampot (San Miguel Chapultepec) for elegant, breathable occasion dresses.

Afternoon

Department Store Browse: El Palacio de Hierro Polanco (broadest variety under one roof for eveningwear, cocktail dresses, and accessories).

Evening

✨ Open Evening: Pick spontaneously from the Food Pool (e.g. Antolina or El Parnita) or have dinner with friends.

Day 4

Wednesday, Nov 4 • Centro Histórico

History & Courtyards
Morning

Historic Core: Zócalo, Metropolitan Cathedral, Templo Mayor ruins, and the golden interior of Palacio Postal.

Lunch

🍽️ Relaxed lunch option: Heritage classics at El Cardenal or courtyard dining under the trees at Azul Histórico.

Evening

✨ Open evening for family and friends.

Day 5

Thursday, Nov 5 • Chapultepec & Condesa

Culture & Park
Morning

Anthropology Museum (MNA): World-renowned collection set inside Bosque de Chapultepec (2–3 hours at an easy pace).

Afternoon

Condesa Wander: Walk the shaded perimeter of Parque México and Avenida Ámsterdam for coffee or ice cream.

Evening

✨ Open evening: Choose casually from Roma/Condesa favorites in the Food Pool.

Day 6

Friday, Nov 6 • Excursion & Lucha Libre

Fight Night 🥊
Morning

Flexible Excursion: Early morning trip to Teotihuacán Pyramids (leave by 07:30 to beat crowds) OR an early food run at Central de Abasto.

Afternoon

Rest, nap, and recharge back at the Coyoacán base.

20:30

🎟️ Lucha Libre: Viernes Espectacular at Arena México: CMLL's premier weekly championship card with high-flying spectacle and unforgettable atmosphere!

Day 7

Saturday, Nov 7 • San Ángel Arts

Boutique & Art Market
Daytime

Bazar El Sábado: Iconic art market in San Ángel with silver jewelry, ceramics, and textiles right next to Coyoacán. Stroll Plaza San Jacinto.

Evening

✨ Open evening with family & friends in southern CDMX (easy walk/ride back to base).

Day 8

Sunday, Nov 8 • Roma Stroll & Farewells

Neighborhood Day
Morning

Roma & Condesa Stroll: Independent boutiques, bookstores, and sidewalk cafés around Parque México.

Lunch

🐟 Long seafood lunch at Contramar.

Evening

✨ Farewell drinks and toast to a memorable week.

Day 9

Monday, Nov 9 • Departure

Check-out
Morning

Final morning coffee & bakery run in Coyoacán. Pack up, check out of loft by 12:00, and transfer to MEX Airport.

Dedicated Dress Shopping Day (Tuesday, Nov 3)

A curated route balancing artisanal Mexican couture with luxury multi-brand department store selection.

Carla Fernández

Calle Marsella 72, Colonia Juárez

Artisan Couture

Celebrated Mexican fashion house collaborating with indigenous weavers and master artisans. Perfect for modern, structured dresses, unique textiles, and statement silhouettes.

Area: Juárez / Roma Open Map →

Yakampot

Salvatierra 45, San Miguel Chapultepec

Boutique

Understated, elegant, and fluid occasion dresses crafted in natural fibers. Celebrates Mexican textile heritage with clean contemporary tailoring. (Closed weekends).

Area: San Miguel Chapultepec Open Map →

El Palacio de Hierro Polanco

Av. Moliere 222, Polanco

Flagship Department Store

Known as "El Palacio de los Palacios". The best one-stop location if you need to try a high volume of formal, cocktail, and wedding-guest dresses across diverse brands and sizes.

Flexible Restaurant Pool (No Fixed Bookings)

Evenings are open. Drop into or book any of these favorite spots across neighborhoods whenever you're ready.

Los Danzantes

Plaza Jardín Centenario 12, Coyoacán

Base Favorite

Directly overlooking the lively plaza in Coyoacán with shaded courtyard tables. Exceptional regional dishes, mole, and artisanal mezcal right by your loft. Ideal for family dinners.

Vibe: Plaza Courtyard • Mid-Range Open Map →

Contramar

Durango 200, Roma Norte

Seafood Classic

Mexico City's legendary seafood lunch spot. Signature tuna tostadas and red-and-green grilled pescado a la talla. (Best for long midday/afternoon meals).

Vibe: Buzzing Lunch • Mid-Range Open Map →

El Cardenal

Calle de la Palma 23, Centro Histórico

Heritage Mexican

A landmark of authentic traditional Mexican cuisine. Handmade tortillas, slow-simmered dishes, and classic hospitality. Superb for a sit-down meal in Centro.

Vibe: Traditional Sit-down • Mid-Range Open Map →

Azul Histórico

Isabel La Católica 30, Centro Histórico

Scenic Palace

Set in the open-air courtyard of a 17th-century palace shaded by laurel trees. Celebrated regional moles, duck buñuelos, and authentic heritage dishes.

Vibe: Courtyard Ambience • Mid-Range Open Map →

El Parnita

Av. Yucatán 84, Roma Norte

Tacos & Antojos

Lively, bustling corner taquería serving creative antojos, pork belly tacos, tempura shrimp, and fresh house salsas. Perfect for a casual high-flavor drop-in bite.

Vibe: Casual & Lively Open Map →

Antolina

Alfonso Reyes 115, Condesa

Chinampa Produce

Intimate sidewalk bistro sourcing organic produce straight from Xochimilco chinampas. Stellar plantain molotes, mole negro, and boutique mezcals without tasting-menu prices.

Vibe: Leafy Sidewalk • Mid-Range Open Map →

Fonda Fina

Medellín 79, Roma Norte

Elevated Comfort

Refined homestyle Mexican comfort cooking. Pick your choice of protein and pair it with house moles and warm heirloom corn tortillas. Warm and unpretentious.

Vibe: Cozy Dining • Mid-Range Open Map →

El Califa de León

Av. Ribera de San Cosme 56, San Rafael

Legendary Taco Stand

The historic stand famous for its Gaonera taco—thin, tender beef fillet cooked on a sizzling flat top with fresh lime and salt. Sidewalk counter eating only.

Vibe: Stand-and-eat Street Food Open Map →

El Pescadito / La Pezciosa

Calle Atlixco 38, Condesa

Sonora Seafood

Generous battered fish, shrimp, and cheese-stuffed chile poblano tacos with an expansive self-serve fresh salsa bar. Fast, delicious, and budget-friendly.

Vibe: Quick Casual Tacos Open Map →
